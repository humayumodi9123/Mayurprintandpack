MAYUR PRINT & PACK — FINAL HOSTINGER PACKAGE
Domain: https://www.mayurprint.in/

HOSTINGER:
1. Open File Manager > public_html
2. Take a backup of the existing live website.
3. Delete/replace the old website files inside public_html.
4. Upload this ZIP and Extract directly inside public_html.
5. Confirm index.html, assets folder, robots.txt, sitemap.xml and .htaccess are directly inside public_html.
6. Open https://www.mayurprint.in/ and hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5).
7. In Google Search Console submit: https://www.mayurprint.in/sitemap.xml

Performance:
- Embedded base64 images were extracted to /assets/images/
- Images were resized/converted to WebP
- Gallery/product images use lazy loading
- Browser caching and gzip rules are included
